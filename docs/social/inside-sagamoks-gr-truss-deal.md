## LinkedIn

Three records tell the beginning of Sagamok's GR Truss story: a $100 purchase in the audited statements, $900,000 in Council approvals, and an Ontario security filing.

New RHT Circle investigation by Laura Owl. The filing connects GR Truss equipment to the same numbered company that sold Sagamok the South Market property for $7 million. Council minutes show a $500,000 advance at 4 per cent, repayable by March 2026. The minutes held through May 19 record no repayment, extension or current balance.

The piece is careful about its own limits. The latest audit is clean, and its year ended before the advance was approved, so it cannot answer the repayment question. The records establish the approvals, the dates and the registered overlap. They do not establish wrongdoing. Five specific documents would settle the story, and the article names each one.

As the piece puts it: members do not need a theory about what happened. They need the records showing whether the money came back.
