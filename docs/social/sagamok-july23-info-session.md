## LinkedIn

About 70 people, including many Elders, attended the Sagamok member information session on July 23.

The full recording is now up. I explained why I started examining Sagamok's businesses, what the available records show, what remains unanswered, and what members have done to obtain records and independent review.

The discussion covered Sagamok's companies, Council and enterprise roles, borrowing, money provided to Nation-owned companies, the members' records request, outside oversight submissions, and the Sagamok Members' Accountability Resolution.

This is what member-led accountability looks like: public records, plain explanation, and a room full of people asking questions.
